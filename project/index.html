About Lagos Island

Lagos Island, the historic heart of Lagos, Nigeria, is a living museum of architectural heritage. From the grandeur of colonial-era buildings to the unique Afro-Brazilian structures built by freed slaves returning from Brazil, the island offers a fascinating journey through Nigeria's history.

This guide showcases 6 significant landmarks that represent the diverse architectural styles and historical periods that have shaped Lagos Island.
